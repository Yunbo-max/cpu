# KVC-B: future attention learned from full-cache traces vs compressed rollouts
Screened only; NOT a tree node, focus or original algorithm.
Apple KVP ICML26 source derives reward from reverse-cumulative future attention of precomputed uncompressed traces. In a deliberately abstract two-world toy, full-cache observations are identical while the better budget1 action A/B is reversed. Under logs-only access, minimax regret=.5, a standard causal-support limitation. It is NOT a demonstration of regret for KVP's actual LLM and not an unclaimed theorem.
