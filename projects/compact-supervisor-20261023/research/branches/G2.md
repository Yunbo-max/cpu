# G2: higher order joint-failure interaction
Mechanism: order of information available about correlated failures.
A unit-capacity source bottleneck feeds four monitored paths in A and
four monitored plus one hidden spare path in B. The two graphs have
max flow one for all 15 subsets with zero to three monitored failures.
Deleting all four edges makes the flows zero and one respectively.
Even perfect lower-order queries do not identify the fourth-order query.
This is elementary combinatorial indistinguishability, not new graph
theory. Check ITCS multi-edge sensitivity oracles and higher-order
network cascade papers before any novel design claim.
Next: examine held-out network failure set interactions under a
strong fair multi-failure oracle.
Falsifier: existing published baseline already suffices at equal
bits/latency or no incremental high-order effect in real networks.
