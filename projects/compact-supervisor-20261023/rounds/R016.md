## Source-backed decisions
- QED original *The Query Knows What to Forget*, arXiv:2608.13668v1, Section 3.1 explicitly states the gate exists to prevent a correction erasing content needed by **future reads**. Section 3.2 Eqs. 11-12 already give the exact state-change mechanism used in L1; the future-probe projection is elementary algebra, not a new theorem.
- QED Sections 4.2 and A.1 / Table 4 ALREADY test **same trained weights** with strength factors s=(-1,0,.5,1,1.5,2) applied during the whole prefill and decoding recurrence, not just the final read. At 16K S-NIAH-1 seed0: 41.4% s=0 vs 41.8% s=1; seed1: 55.0% s=0 vs 65.6% s=1. Therefore the broad claim 'same-checkpoint online erasure can change long retrieval' is not our novelty, and causal reliance is seed-dependent.
- QED learned mean per-head strength at s=1: 0.101 and 0.120 for the two seeds (not maximal `lambda_max=0.25`). R015 used lambda=.25. When those **reported means are inserted as stress-test scalars** into its frozen-state 2D model with p=.1,rho=.6,beta=.8, both yield aggregate IMPROVEMENT rather than harm (Delta R=-.00229452 and -.00185205). For p=.05 the same toy yields small harm (+.000941903, +.002023096). These are NOT measured head-specific gates nor actual LM outcomes.
- A controlled later state transition with A=I-kk^T erases the earlier QED-vs-baseline delta completely, showing fixed-state collateral does not imply lasting delayed fact loss. Under common scalar decay alpha=.8, five subsequent steps attenuate the difference by alpha^5=.32768. This exact propagation assumes same future operators and writes across the branches, not a full nonlinear trained LM.
- Close causal prior: *How Linear Attention Remembers*, arXiv:2609.33093, already quantifies non-target fact break (0.4%-1.4%) and aligns subsequent keys to produce non-target margin decrease (about 0.27-0.44 logits) on pretrained GLA/GDN. It does NOT run QED-specific future-query erasure, but removes the broad originality claim that updates can damage other facts.
- Public QED official source and matching trained checkpoints NOT verified by primary-paper resources, GitHub repository search or targeted web/HF discovery. The Hugging Face model-search connector returned a tool-unavailable error; do not report exhaustive Hub absence or treat public GDN2 code or independent GDN weights as QED weights.
