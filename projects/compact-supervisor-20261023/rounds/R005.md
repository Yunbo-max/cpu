## Key new source-grounded correction to R004
The primary Frozen Full KV controller is not a free ||c||2<=1 model.
Actual four K/V projections (layers 14+27) each use rank16 and full mixing:
A_s=alpha*R_s/(1+||R_s||F), with alpha_max=3. The effective A_s ranges over
an open Frobenius ball of radius 3; the raw coefficients are unbounded.
The exact inverse A -> R=A/(3-||A||F) was checked on 100 fixed random
matrices, with maximum round-trip error 1.68754e-14.
This fixes the interpretation of R004's illustrative radius-one proof
without rewriting its historical record.

## New exact mechanism: dead excitation despite full gradient coverage
At one module, delta Z = M*(ZB) A B^T. Gradient projection GB (measured by
the old rho) may be perfectly aligned, but ZB can vanish. Then for all
allowed effective A, the perturbation is exactly zero at this module.
With a fixed upstream state, the derivative dmargin/dA is
[(M*ZB)^T (G_margin B)], the product of projected activations and projected
score-margin gradients, NOT gradient overlap alone. A simple two-vector toy
shows identical rho=1, kappa=1 and identical initial margins, but only the
nonzero-excitation case is correctable by this edit.
One dead module alone does not imply failure of other adapted modules.
No real VLM efficacy or output certificate is implied.

## Second exact mechanism: shared-controller feasibility
Three margins [-.5+a, -.5+a, -.5-a] with |a|<3 are individually reachable,
rho=1, and aggregate positive direction agreement kappa=1; no single a
corrects all. Max 2/3 correct on the toy.
For general first-order candidate-score margins m_i=b_i+sum_s<G_is,A_s>,
the exact max-min shared-margin supremum is
min over lambda simplex [sum_i lambda_i b_i+
                          sum_s alpha_s ||sum_i lambda_i G_is||F].
A lambda upper value <=0 certifies no single bounded controller gives
all selected examples positive margins. This uses elementary convex duality.
It is not a novel theorem or a practical adaptation algorithm by itself.

## Actual EventTune evaluation function
src/eventttt/task_qwen.py scores each candidate by a SUM of teacher-forced
target-token log probabilities and predicts by argmax of corresponding
probabilities. Thus certificates must differentiate these sequence
candidate-score margins; single raw next-token logit estimates are incorrect.
