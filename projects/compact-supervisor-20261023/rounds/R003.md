## Algebra and falsifiable prediction
Scalar Adam at a fixed time/state is
F(g) = -lr * kappa * (A+B*g) / (sqrt(C+D*g^2)+e),
A=beta1*m_prev; B=1-beta1; C=beta2*v_prev; D=1-beta2;
kappa=sqrt(1-beta2^t)/(1-beta1^t); e=epsilon*sqrt(1-beta2^t).
For C>0 the update is smooth (including near zero gradient), and for a mean-zero delta, Taylor's integral remainder gives
|E[F(q+delta)]-F(q)| <= 0.5 * sup_{g in observed segment union}|F''(g)| * Var(delta).
Writing s=sqrt(C+D*g^2), s'=D*g/s, s''=D*C/s^3,
F''(g)= -lr*kappa * [-2B*s'/(s+e)^2 - (A+B*g)*s''/(s+e)^2 + 2(A+B*g)*(s')^2/(s+e)^3].
In contrast at t=1 and v0=0, epsilon small gives F(g) approximately -lr*sign(g); its lack of a uniformly small curvature scale near g=0 makes sign crossings dominant.
Falsifier: with fixed matched C>0, bounded g and variance shrunk toward zero, a persistent O(lr) nonvanishing gap would violate the smooth-state prediction, indicating code/assumption error or changed update timing. This is a conditional mathematical statement, not a novel algorithm or a trained-model result.
