Its exact subgradient is g=2[w*(c_fixed)-w*(2hatc-c_fixed)]. w*(c) means the optimizer minimizer at c, not a model prediction. For W={e1,e2}, c_obs=(0,Delta), U=L2 ball radius rho, Delta>0 and observed w_obs=e1, static label cost c_fixed=(rho,Delta). It switches from e1 to e2 iff Delta<4eta (for non-tie).
