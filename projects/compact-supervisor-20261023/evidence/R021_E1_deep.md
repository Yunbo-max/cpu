## D2. Source-exact forward rule and conditional math
In the Triton code: n=∑k_i²; a=∑b_i k_i²; ν=∑b_i²k_i²; r²=max(nν−a²,0); c²=exp(−2g_max); rcrit²=max((c²−1)(1−(1−a)²/c²),0).
Only when unsafe=(r²>rcrit²) does the guard change b. Then μ=a/n and η=clip(sqrt((1−M)rcrit²/r²),0,1), M=1/32; b_eff=μ+η(b−μ). A quantization re-test can fall back to scalar mean. This is a branch-and-shrink, NOT a continuous Euclidean projection when M>0.
Take k=(1,1)/sqrt2, α=.98, Sbar=αe1, b(t)=(.5+t,.5−t), 0<t<.5. Then n=1,a=.5,r²=t²,c²=α^(−2). Define T=sqrt(rcrit²)=0.17701078966683267 and τ=sqrt((1−M)rcrit²)=0.17422304395271113. Exact source condition means:
  t≤T: effective contrast t.
  t>T: effective contrast τ.
There is a one-sided jump of T−τ=0.002787745714... at t=T. For probe y=e2^T S_new with zero write and initial S=e1: y(t)=−α/2*(.5+effective contrast). Upward jump magnitude α/2*(T−τ)=0.001365995400 as t crosses T. R020 INCORRECTLY stated t>τ is entirely flat. Correct flat fibre starts only when t>T, not t>τ; the intervening (τ,T] region remains unprojected. An alternative continuous **stricter** clamp effective contrast min(t,τ) is continuous but modifies more inputs and is not an established method.
