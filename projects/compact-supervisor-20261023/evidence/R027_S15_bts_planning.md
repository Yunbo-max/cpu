D2: For plan j, BTS(j)=(1/3) Σ_a log((A_a(j)+eps)/(P_a(j)+eps)), with P_a mean of other-score forecasts. If own scores are h>l>m≥1 and all three agents predict high h, low m, then BTS_high=0, BTS_low=log(l/m)>0, hence low own-feasibility plan wins, independent of true feasibility. This is an algebraic scoring inversion under *inaccurate second-order beliefs*, not an actual LLM task failure.
D3: Opposing negative control: set forecasts exactly equal to own scores; both BTS=0, mean feasibility tie-break chooses high plan. Standard absolute confidence constraints, calibration, or evidential verification can prevent toy misranking, but tradeoffs exist. The paper already includes epistemic refinement after plan selection.
