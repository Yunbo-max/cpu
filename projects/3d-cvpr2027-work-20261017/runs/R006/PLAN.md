# R006: Is there an oracle headroom for unknown-focal gravity/oriented pose?

## Parent problem and decision

The desired inference problem is absolute 2D–3D camera pose with unknown query focal length, real measured gravity of uncertain reliability and oriented image features. The proposed adaptive real-IMU method does not exist yet; R004 invalidated the old measured-prior interface and R005 found weak window-only reliability features. Before developing a new method, test a **necessary upper-bound condition**: if an ideal, ground-truth-derived gravity/tilt prior fed to the published UP2PfORI solver cannot show an advantage over a source-qualified, focal-unknown P4Pf on the same real correspondences at informative outlier/overlap regimes, this route has little headroom. An advantage would only justify further work; it is not a measured-IMU method result.

UP2PfORI is the original 2026 author solver and already reports synthetic IMU noise and comparisons against P4Pf/P3.5Pf. The separate CVPR 2020 gDLS* weights noisy gravity priors, and ICCV 2023 Pautrat et al. combine rough-gravity solvers. No sample-size or weighted-prior novelty is claimed. This R006 is source/baseline qualification, an M/R development audit of an existing method, not creation of a new candidate or independent paper confirmation.

## Derivation and falsifiable prediction

For inlier fraction `w`, a minimal solver drawing `s` correspondences succeeds at least once in `N` independent draws with probability `1-(1-w^s)^N`. UP2PfORI has `s=2` with ideal known tilt and oriented features; unknown-focal P4Pf has `s=4` and no gravity. At `w=0.5`, 95%-success sample counts are approximately 11 and 47. The smaller sample can matter at high outlier rates, but P4Pf may recover more stable geometry or focal length when image orientation/normal estimates are poor. The original paper has already studied this generic sample-size effect; here the unknown is whether the inherited simple RGB-D front end gives enough real-feature headroom at all. Prediction: any oracle advantage should grow as the empirical correspondence inlier fraction falls and be visible under equal measured CPU, not merely equal hypothesis count. Falsifier: no oracle advantage on paired rotation/failure/time–accuracy metrics after qualification, or an advantage confined to GT leakage without a viable sensor-derived interface. A positive result does not establish real-IMU efficacy.

The two gravity directions alone impose `R g_r=g_q`, leaving a one-parameter rotation family. The old solver instead expects `R=R_y(theta) R_xz` with a fixed `R_xz`, and R004 showed its arbitrary-reference-camera `R_xz` is not identified by that pair of directions. Therefore the oracle arm is explicitly an unattainable information upper bound in this wrapper; P4Pf is the focal-unknown visual comparator, not an information-matched measured-IMU comparison.

## Prospective implementation and resource calibration

Keep the exact R001 Harris/patch/normal feature construction, mutual ratio matching, principal point and 3-pixel score; read official TUM fr2/xyz native RGB/depth/GT. Use PoseLib's `p4pf` source (Kukelova et al., CVPR 2016) in the same native C++ wrapper; both arms estimate query focal. Ground truth enters only the oracle `Rxz` and scoring. First compile and calibrate on 12 timestamp-selected pairs with reference times spanning seconds 10–110 and offsets 0.15, 0.30, 0.60, 1.20 seconds (three references × four offsets); accept timestamp miss ≤20 ms, and qualify ≥24 correspondences. Confirm finite poses, pair parsing, focal output, source hashes, CPU per pair and peak RSS. The full cohort size and finite batch are frozen **after** that resource calibration, before its run. Do not silently retune the solver or select easy pairs.

For the full R006 design, evaluate multiple time offsets and matched 512-hypothesis pools, recording all skipped/failed cases. Compare rotation, camera center, focal, failure, inlier count, runtime and paired distributions; stratify by empirical GT reprojection-inlier fraction only **after** evaluation. A one-hour meaningful CPU budget is a target, not a stopwatch: maximize distinct useful pair/condition coverage within 4 GB address space, one numerical thread, bounded wall tasks; stop or switch question if the prerequisite fails. No repeated identical evaluations to consume time. Every subprocess has an exit/status/output/CPU/RSS receipt. A/B/C execution checks apply across source/claim, actual execution, and cross-call continuity; they do not replace these scientific steps.
